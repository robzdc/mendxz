## About Astrosurf

**Astrosurf** is a one-tap arcade game about surfing through space, one planet at a time. Your rocket circles a planet. Tap at the right moment and it launches in a straight line toward the next one. Hit the planet's ring and you're caught in a new orbit. Miss, and you drift off into the dark.

It takes one second to learn and much longer to master. Every planet climbs higher, rings get smaller, orbits get faster, and some planets start drifting side to side. A run lasts a minute or two, so it fits into any break.

## How scoring works

- **+1** for every planet you reach.
- **Close orbit:** get caught close to a planet's surface for a bonus, and chain close orbits for more.
- **Skip a planet:** fly past one planet straight into the next for extra points.
- **Points:** every run pays points based on your score and difficulty. Spend them on rockets and music in the shop.

## Who made it

Astrosurf is designed and built by Roberto del Castillo, a developer with 10+ years of experience, under [mendxz](/), a hobby studio in Mexico City. It's the first game Roberto has made, built for fun with AI as part of the toolkit.
